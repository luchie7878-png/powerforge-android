1. Who We Are

PowerForge ("we", "us", "our") operates the PowerForge platform, accessible at powerforge.abacusai.app and via our Android application on Google Play. PowerForge provides online strength and fitness training programmes.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at: privacy@powerforge.app

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use PowerForge:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form) when you register.
  • Profile data: fitness goals, training history, and programme progress that you choose to enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session duration, and click patterns, collected automatically.
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, device model, and IP address.
  • Payment information: if you purchase a programme, payment details are processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store card numbers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and training dashboard.
  • Deliver the training programmes and learning content you access.
  • Process payments and send receipts.
  • Send service-related communications (e.g. account confirmations, updates to programmes).
  • Improve the platform through aggregated, anonymised usage analysis.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK & EU Users)

For users in the United Kingdom and European Economic Area, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: improving platform security and performance, and preventing fraud.
  • Legal obligation: complying with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Consent: for any optional communications such as newsletters (you can withdraw consent at any time).

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate PowerForge, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Hosting & infrastructure: Abacus.AI (platform hosting).
  • Payments: Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant payment processing).
  • Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics (privacy-preserving; no cookies or cross-site tracking).

All third-party providers are required to protect your data and may not use it for their own marketing purposes.

6. Cookies

PowerForge uses strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and remember your session. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set a performance cookie; this does not identify you personally.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a structured format).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in the UK: the ICO).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@powerforge.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored in plain text. Access to personal data within our team is limited to those who need it to provide the service.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If we become aware of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. Children

PowerForge is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here with an updated date. For significant changes we will notify registered users by email.
